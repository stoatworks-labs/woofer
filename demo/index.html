Photosensitivity warning

The Height view flashes with loud music, this page's own track included. It shows the cone's stroke as grey across the whole picture, and every kick brightens and darkens it. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, stay in the Lit view.

Broadcast and web guidance (Ofcom's, and WCAG's) puts the line at three flashes in any second, six rises and falls in brightness. Measured from the plugin's own output with xenon's rule (the whole frame and each third of it, at 60 fps): on this page's track at the default Drive the Height view made 10 rises and falls in its worst second over the whole picture and 17 in its worst third (16 and 21 at full Drive, +30 dB); over 33 of Resolume's demo clips, with music mastered to about −9 dBFS RMS, it went over the line on 10, at worst 22. The Lit view, the plugin's default, stayed under the line in every one of those runs, at the default Drive and at full Drive, and never raised a clip's own flash rate.

The page opens in the Lit view, and no preset uses the Height view. A link that opens in it opens paused, and a caution line under the picture says so whenever View is Height. The status lamp, a small LED below the driver, flashes white each time the coil hits its stop, which the track's loudest kicks do; the Lit figures include it. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
